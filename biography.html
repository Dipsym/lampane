A little about me

Biography.

I am a PhD researcher in particle physics phenomenology at the School of Physics, IISER Thiruvananthapuram, where I work under the supervision of Dr. Tanumoy Mandal. My research explores physics beyond the Standard Model, with an emphasis on effective field theory, varying Higgs and top-quark couplings, vector leptoquarks, and collider phenomenology. I am interested in how new interactions can be formulated theoretically and tested through collider measurements and precision observables. My work combines analytical calculations with computational tools to connect particle-physics models to data.
