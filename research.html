The questions I work on

Research themes.

My work is in particle physics phenomenology: taking theoretical models beyond the Standard Model and asking how their effects could be tested through collider processes and precision observables.

01 / EFT

Varying couplings & effective field theory

Studying frameworks where scalar fields dynamically modify the Higgs self-coupling and the top-quark Yukawa coupling. The phenomenology includes scalar production and decays, di-Higgs and di-top resonances, and multi-Higgs final states.

EFTScalar sectorsHiggs physics
02 / LEPTOQUARKS

Vector leptoquarks & lepton dipole moments

Investigating how vector leptoquarks contribute to the magnetic and electric dipole moments of charged leptons, including connections to low-energy measurements and collider constraints.

U₁ & V₂Muon g−2EDMs
03 / COLLIDERS

Collider phenomenology

Connecting BSM interactions to testable signatures with cross-section calculations, resonance searches, Higgs-pair production, top-quark processes and future-collider projections.

LHCHL-LHCMonte Carlo
04 / PRECISION

Precision constraints

Using precision observables and existing experimental measurements to restrict parameter spaces of new-physics models and identify promising complementary probes.

EW precisionLoop calculations
05 / COMPUTATION

Symbolic and numerical methods

Combining analytic calculations and numerical tools to translate Lagrangians and amplitudes into phenomenological predictions.

MathematicaFeynCalcPackage-X
06 / SIMULATION

Collider event studies

Using event-generation and simulation tools to study the connection between theoretical processes and experimentally observable signatures.
