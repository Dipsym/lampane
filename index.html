01 / ABOUT

Curious about what lies
beyond the known.

My work sits at the intersection of theoretical ideas and experimentally testable signals: building models, calculating their consequences, and asking where collider and precision data can reveal new physics.

I am a PhD researcher in particle physics phenomenology at the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research Thiruvananthapuram (IISER TVM), working with Dr. Tanumoy Mandal.

My research focuses on physics beyond the Standard Model, with an emphasis on effective field theory, new scalar interactions, vector leptoquarks and collider signatures. I am especially interested in how theoretical extensions can be constrained through precision observables and processes at high-energy colliders.

I also work with computational tools for phenomenology—from symbolic calculations to event generation and detector-level studies.

02 / RESEARCH

Questions that drive
the work.

A few themes that connect my current research and previous work.

RESEARCH AREA 01

Effective field theory & new scalar interactions

Exploring EFT extensions in which a scalar field modifies Higgs self-interactions and the top Yukawa coupling, and studying their possible signatures in Higgs-pair and top-pair production.

RESEARCH AREA 02

Vector leptoquarks & lepton dipole moments

Investigating how vector leptoquarks affect the magnetic and electric dipole moments of charged leptons, and how these scenarios stand against collider and low-energy constraints.

RESEARCH AREA 03

Collider phenomenology & precision tests

Connecting new-physics models to measurable quantities through cross-section calculations, electroweak precision observables and phenomenological studies of collider data.
